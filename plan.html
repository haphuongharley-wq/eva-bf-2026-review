Black Friday VSL campaign — overall plan

Contract

  • Outcome: People who register receive a new VSL from Eva and can buy MiddelhavsMagi at 4.997 kr on Wed 25.11 (offer 1) or 5.997 kr on Thu 26.11 (offer 2). Every buyer gets the Julegave-pakke (Julens Gavepakke repackaged, stated value 997 kr) via a festive gift landing page.
  • Offer structure: price ladder, same as the summer VSL. Reference prices: full 9.997 kr, webinar evergreen 6.997 kr.
  • Scope: new VSL script; Kajabi pages (registration, thank-you, one video/sales page, gift landing page); repackaged gift: five digital booklets (Julemagasinet 75 p. / 70 recipes, Julegaver til å spise opp 14 p., Pynt til jul 12 p., Restefest 22 p., Fra sukkerkick til energiboost 12 p.); the Christmas menu, shopping list and recipe cards are dropped; email sequence; 6 sales SMS; one live Q&A. The offer includes the 3 existing bonuses (14 dagers kroppsreset, Fra krasj til ro, Kutt matutgiftene). Creative angle: the Norwegian winter calendar (advent → julebord → julaften → romjul → lang januar); start before first Sunday of Advent (29.11).
  • Audience: Eva's email list, excluding existing customers and anyone who registered for a webinar in the last 30 days. Registration stays open during both sales days.
  • Constraints: copy guardrails in README.md; all times Oslo; one edpay offer per price; the buy button always charges the price shown.
  • Non-goals: Meta ads and ad copy, changes to the webinar funnel or /kurs.
  • Acceptance: all pages and emails live in Norwegian; prices and deadlines switch automatically; every buy button charges the price shown; buyers can unlock and download the gift PDFs until 31.12.2026; the sales page shows a closed state from 27.11 00:00.

Live Q&A — recommendation

Thursday 26.11, 16:00–16:45 Oslo, Zoom (or Facebook group live), for everyone registered who has not bought.

Why this slot sells the most: - Day 1 is carried by the new video and the lowest price. Day 2 has no new reason to buy except "the door is still open" — a live session gives that reason and reaches the people who watched but hesitated. - 16:00 is Eva's choice; the replay is up from ca. 17:00, so people who cannot join live watch it the same evening, with about seven hours left before the 23:59 deadline. - Questions answered live are the real objections (time, family food, Klarna, "is it for me at 60+"), handled by Eva in her own voice; that is what converts fence-sitters.

Run sheet (45 min): 1. 0–5: welcome, who is here, one quick win (a flavour grip they can use tonight). 2. 5–30: answer questions; Eva prepares answers to the 6 most common objections in advance. 3. 30–38: what MiddelhavsMagi contains, the gift, the guarantee, price 5.997 until 23:59. 4. 38–45: last questions; link in chat; "døren lukkes kl. 23:59".

Supporting emails: invitation in the 25.11 evening email and the 26.11 08:00 email, a "starter om 1 time" at 15:00 (SMS 15:00 and 15:45), and the replay link in the 17:00 A9 and 21:00 last-call emails.

Overlap with the 22.11 webinar funnel

A webinar runs on Sun 22.11; its automated encores (Mon–Wed 19:00) and its sales sequence at 6.997 kr run in the same days as BF at 4.997 / 5.997 kr. Automation removes the workload clash, not the price clash: - Price gap seen by the same person. Webinar registrants are excluded from BF emails, but only if the exclusion is evaluated at send time. Someone who registers for the 22.11 webinar after a one-off export would get both. → Use a dynamic segment (tag-based), not a static list. - Refunds and re-buys. Anyone who buys at 6.997 kr on 22.–25.11 is inside the 14-day guarantee and may refund and re-buy at 4.997 kr if they see the BF price (forwarded email, Facebook group). → Keep BF out of public channels; agree a policy in advance (e.g. honour the difference with the gift package rather than a refund). - /kurs on Thu 26.11 shows 9.997 kr while BF sells at 5.997 kr — only a problem if someone sees both; the dynamic exclusion covers it. - Q&A timing: Thursday 16:00 does not overlap an encore (encores run Mon–Wed).

Risks

  • Gift value claim. The same content is sold publicly at 297 kr on /julepakke. Calling the repackaged version "verdi 997 kr" while the 297 kr page is live is a misleading reference price under Norwegian marketing rules. Before using 997 kr: take down or rework /julepakke, and make the repackaging substantial (new layout, covers, extra recipes or printables) so the value is defensible.
  • Gift gate is light protection. A form that reveals download links on the page keeps casual visitors out but not determined ones (PDF URLs can be shared). Acceptable for a gift; use Kajabi-hosted files and unlisted URLs.
  • Gift expiry. The page can switch to "closed" after 31.12.2026, but PDF URLs keep working until the files are removed — schedule removal on 1.1.2027.
  • Video not ready by 17.11 — the schedule has no slack: script approval on 13.11 and recording on 14.–15.11 are the critical path. If Eva cannot record that weekend, the video can slip to 23.11 without moving the 25.11 release (registration and emails do not need the video), but that breaks the "all set up before 18.11" rule.
  • Buy button charges the wrong price — separate edpay offers, a test purchase per price.
  • Claims — the summer page's "25 % magefett / 53 % Alzheimer" and weight wording must not be reused.

Phase 2 — VSL script

Requirements

  • Norwegian, Eva speaking in first person, warm and low-pressure; 15–20 minutes (summer video was "under 20 minutter").
  • Longevity angle and guardrails from README.md: matglede, "legg til, ikke kutt", "de beste årene ligger foran deg"; no weight, no banned words, no invented stories or numbers.
  • Only verified sources for any study claim (e.g. PREDIMED); no "25 % belly fat" / "53 % Alzheimer" claims.

Seasonal angle (approved)

  • Promise (A): "Jul uten januar-angeren" — enjoy all of Christmas (julebord, syv slag, ribbe, pinnekjøtt) and go into the new year without a January diet. The reason to start now: begin before Christmas, not on 1 January. Frame around energy, feeling good and matglede, never weight loss.
  • Pain (B): mørketid — dark afternoons, tiredness, sweet cravings, wanting "kos"; versus winter in Provence full of colour (citrus season, new olive oil, herbs). No mood or health promises, no supplement advice.
  • Story (C): Christmas in Provence — Gros souper and the 13 desserts as cultural colour; links naturally to the Christmas gift package. How Eva herself celebrates must be confirmed by Eva.
  • Use widely recognised Norwegian seasonal patterns (julebord season, førjulsstress, the January-diet cycle); no statistics without a source.

Proposed structure

  1. Hook (0–1 min): the December → January-diet cycle; "this year it can be different".
  2. Pain: mørketid and førjulsstress — tired afternoons, cravings, hosting the whole family.
  3. Eva's story (only facts Eva confirms): Norwegian, 14 years in Saint Jeannet; winter and Christmas in Provence (Gros souper, 13 desserts).
  4. Why diets don't last — and why January diets fail fastest; what the women in Provence do instead ("legg til, ikke kutt").
  5. Teaching: the four flavour grips (syre, godt fett, urter og krydder, tekstur) applied to the Christmas season — one concrete winter dish and one julebord tip.
  6. What changes when you eat this way: steadier energy through the dark season, enjoying the Christmas table, starting January without a diet (no weight promises).
  7. Offer: MiddelhavsMagi contents, 12 months with Eva, guarantee; the Christmas gift package (value 997 kr): five digital booklets — Julemagasinet, Julegaver til å spise opp, Pynt til jul, Restefest, Fra sukkerkick til energiboost (no Christmas menu / shopping list / recipe cards).
  8. Black Friday terms: 4.997 kr today only, 5.997 kr tomorrow, then closed; live Q&A Thursday 16:00 (replay the same evening).
  9. CTA + close: "Start before Christmas — not on 1 January."

Note: one video is used on both days, so step 7 must work for both (e.g. "i dag og i morgen", with the page showing the current price).

Deliverables

  • vsl-script-no.md (script) and, if needed, vsl-script-vi.md for internal review.
  • Slide/b-roll notes per section.

Validation

  • Eva approves facts and wording before recording.
  • Guardrail check: grep script for banned words and weight terms.
